# Body Measures & Lifestyle Calculators Reference

## 1. Body Mass Index (BMI)
- **Formula:** $\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight (kg)}}{(\text{Height (m)})^2}$
- **NHS Bands:** Underweight (<18.5), Healthy weight (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25.0–29.9), Obesity class 1 (30.0–34.9), Obesity class 2 (35.0–39.9), Severe obesity (>=40.0).

## 2. Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR)
- **Reference:** Ashwell M, et al. *Obes Rev.* 2012;13(3):275-286.
- **Formula:** $\text{WHtR} = \frac{\text{Waist Circumference (cm)}}{\text{Height (cm)}}$
- **Guidance:** NICE advises keeping waist circumference under half your height ($\text{WHtR} < 0.5$).

## 3. Body Roundness Index (BRI)
- **Reference:** Thomas DM, et al. *Obesity.* 2013;21(11):2264-2271.
- **Formula:** $\text{BRI} = 364.2 - 365.5 \times \sqrt{1 - \frac{(\text{Waist}/(2\pi))^2}{(0.5 \times \text{Height})^2}}$ (Waist and height in identical units).

## 4. Body Surface Area (BSA - Mosteller)
- **Formula:** $\text{BSA} = \sqrt{\frac{\text{Height (cm)} \times \text{Weight (kg)}}{3600}}\,\text{m}^2$

## 5. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR - Mifflin-St Jeor)
- **Reference:** Mifflin MD, et al. *Am J Clin Nutr.* 1990;51(2):241-247.
- **Formula:**
  - Male: $10 \times \text{kg} + 6.25 \times \text{cm} - 5 \times \text{Age} + 5$
  - Female: $10 \times \text{kg} + 6.25 \times \text{cm} - 5 \times \text{Age} - 161$
- **Framing:** Non-prescriptive estimate only.

## 6. UK Alcohol Units
- **Formula:** $\text{Units} = \frac{\text{Volume (mL)} \times \text{ABV (\%)}}{1000}$
- **Guidance:** UK Chief Medical Officers' low-risk benchmark is no more than 14 units weekly.

## 7. Salt & Sodium Converter
- **Formula:** $\text{Salt (g)} = \frac{\text{Sodium (mg)} \times 2.5}{1000}$
- **Guidance:** NHS adult maximum recommendation is 6g of salt (2,400mg sodium) daily.

## 8. Physical Activity (MET-minutes)
- **Formula:** $\text{MET-minutes} = \text{MET level} \times \text{Duration (minutes)}$
- **Guidance:** Benchmark is 150 minutes of moderate activity (500–1000 MET-mins) weekly.

## 9. Plasma Osmolality (Learning Tool)
- **Formula:** $\text{Osmolality} = 2 \times \text{Na}^+ + \text{Glucose} + \text{Urea}\,(\text{mmol/L})$
- **Framing:** Educational learning tool only; hidden by default.
