# Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (ACR) & KDIGO Risk Grid

**Clinical Guidelines:** NICE NG203 / KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline  

---

## 1. ACR Staging Categories

| Stage | ACR ($\text{mg/mmol}$) | Description | Clinical Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | $< 3$ | Normal to mildly increased | Standard annual review in low risk |
| **A2** | $3 - 30$ | Moderately increased (Microalbuminuria) | Recheck to confirm; consider ACEi/ARB or SGLT2i |
| **A3** | $> 30$ | Severely increased (Macroalbuminuria / Nephrotic) | Accelerated risk of progression; nephrology consultation |
