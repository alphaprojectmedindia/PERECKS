# Blood Pressure Staging & 7-Day Home Averaging (NICE NG136)

**Clinical Guideline:** NICE NG136 (Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management)  

---

## 1. Blood Pressure Staging Thresholds

| Stage | Clinic BP Threshold ($\text{mmHg}$) | Home / ABPM Threshold ($\text{mmHg}$) |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal / Target** | $< 140 / 90$ (or $< 130 / 80$ if ACR $\ge 30$ in CKD) | $< 135 / 85$ |
| **Stage 1 Hypertension** | $\ge 140 / 90$ | $\ge 135 / 85$ |
| **Stage 2 Hypertension** | $\ge 160 / 100$ | $\ge 150 / 95$ |
| **Stage 3 / Severe** | $\ge 180 \text{ systolic}$ OR $\ge 120 \text{ diastolic}$ | $\ge 180 / 120$ (Urgent medical evaluation) |

---

## 2. NICE 7-Day Home Blood Pressure Averaging Protocol

1. Patient takes 2 consecutive readings (1 minute apart), twice daily (morning and evening), for 7 consecutive days.
2. **Discard Day 1 readings completely.**
3. Calculate the mean systolic and diastolic values across all remaining readings (Days 2 through 7).
4. Compare the 6-day average against home thresholds ($\ge 135/85\,\text{mmHg}$).

## 3. Pulse Pressure Formula
$$\text{Pulse Pressure} = \text{Systolic BP} - \text{Diastolic BP}$$
* Provided as an educational metric illustrating arterial compliance.
