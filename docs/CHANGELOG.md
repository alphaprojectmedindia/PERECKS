## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-02

### Added
- **Milestone M0 Foundation:** Complete repository architecture, TypeScript strict configurations, Vite + React 18 frontend, Tailwind with NHS colour palette, Atkinson Hyperlegible accessible fonts, and `idb` offline-first database.
- **Milestone M1 Data & Trackers:** Real UK treatment costing dataset (`uk_treatment_costs.json`), CoFID UK food composition table (`foods_uk.json`), FSA 14 allergens register (`allergens.json`), clinician-signed pharmacist guidance (`pharmacist_advice.json`), UKKA renal centres (`renal_units_uk.json`), and OHID prevalence data.
- **Milestone M2 Calculators:** 12 evidence-based clinical calculators with published test vectors (CKD-EPI 2021 race-free eGFR, KDIGO ACR staging, KFRE 4-variable, NICE NG136 BP averaging, BMI, WHtR, WHR, BRI, BSA, BMR, alcohol units, salt converter, MET-minutes, Osmolality).
- **Milestone M3 Mind & Safety:** Standardised PHQ-9 & GAD-7 screeners, automated crisis interception overlay, 2-minute breathing/grounding exercises, and 10 interactive behavioural games with BCTTv1 taxonomy.
- **Milestone M4 Reports & Sharing:** Client-side accessible A4 clinician PDF report generator, expiring hashed share link manager with audit logging, and Find Care NHS Service Search/ODS geocoding locator.
- **Milestone M5 Multilingual & Documentation:** Full 6-language translations (English, Polish, Urdu [RTL], Bengali, Punjabi [Gurmukhi], Tamil), pre-moderated community spaces, and comprehensive governance documentation (DCB0129 Clinical Safety, MHRA Regulatory, DPIA, DTAC, WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility, Online Safety, and 3,500-word Literature Review).
