## 2. Competitive Advantages of PERCKS

1. **Focus on Low-Activation & Early Stages:** Capitalises on the key insight from the SMILE-K RCT: digital self-management delivers its largest clinical benefit (+9.2 PAM points) in low-activation users.
2. **Accessible by Design:** Reading age 9–11 and full 6-language representation prevents digital health inequalities.
3. **Clinical Safety & Non-Advisory Assurance:** DCB0129-compliant hazard management with strict crisis screening and clinician-signed pharmacist guidance.
