# How PERCKS Differs from My Kidneys & Me (SMILE-K RCT)

**Summary for Clinical Investigators & Grant Reviewers**  
**Date:** 2026-10-02  

---

### 1. The SMILE-K Finding That Inspired PERCKS
The *My Kidneys & Me* (MK&M) randomised controlled trial (Lightfoot et al., *npj Digital Medicine* 2024;7:1296) evaluated a 10-week digital intervention in 420 adults with CKD Stages 3–4. While the overall trial change in patient activation (+3.1 points) narrowly missed statistical significance, participants with **low baseline activation experienced significant and clinically meaningful improvements (+6.6 to +9.2 points, p<0.001)**.

---

### 2. Five Key Structural Innovations in PERCKS

| Dimension | My Kidneys & Me (MK&M) | PERCKS Kidney Companion | Why This Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Target Population & Upstream Focus** | Predominantly Stage 3b & 4 (advanced pre-dialysis). | **Stages 1–3 and at-risk adults** (hypertension, diabetes, obesity). | Intervening earlier preserves nephrons before irreversible scarring occurs, yielding maximal health and economic ROI. |
| **2. Learning Experience** | Rigid 10-week didactic web curriculum (built on MyDESMOND). | **Calm, bite-sized daily deck ("Today")** (< 2 mins/lesson), voice read-aloud, and 10 interactive micro-games. | Eliminates cognitive overload and dramatically improves engagement for low-activation and low-literacy users. |
| **3. Whole-Person & Mental Health** | Physical health and walking focus. No integrated mental health screener. | **Integrated PHQ-9, GAD-7, sleep diary, and instant emergency crisis safety screen.** | Addresses the 25–40% prevalence of depression and anxiety in CKD, directly enhancing self-care capability. |
| **4. Linguistic & Health Equity** | English only. | **6 NHS priority languages** (English, Polish, Urdu [RTL], Bengali, Punjabi [Gurmukhi], Tamil). | Directly addresses health inequalities in UK South Asian and ethnic minority populations with 3–5x higher kidney risk. |
| **5. Health Economics & Cost Capture** | No health resource utilisation or UK costing data captured. | **Built-in NHS Treatment Costing dataset** and consent-based health resource use tracker. | Provides immediate health economic data for NICE technology appraisals and NHS commissioning business cases. |
